Softpapaya Services

Small, senior teams that design, build, and run software with you — from the first sketch of an idea to the platform it depends on every day.

Product Design

We turn a rough idea into something you can put in front of users. Discovery workshops narrow the problem, low-fidelity flows test it cheaply, and a small design system carries the result into production without a redesign halfway through the build.

Technologies/Methodologies

Discovery workshops, user interviews, journey mapping, Figma prototypes, component libraries, and usability testing on every other iteration.

Target Audience

Founders validating a first product, and teams whose existing interface has grown faster than anyone's ability to explain it.

Software Development

Senior engineers build and ship the product alongside you, in short iterations with working software at the end of each one. Tests, code review, and continuous delivery are part of the work rather than a phase bolted on at the end.

Technologies/Methodologies

TypeScript, Python, and Go services; React and modern web front ends; PostgreSQL; event-driven integrations; trunk-based development with automated pipelines.

Target Audience

Companies building a new product from scratch, and those modernising a system that still earns its keep but is expensive to change.

DevOps & Kubernetes

We make releases boring. Infrastructure is described in code, environments are reproducible, and the platform your team deploys to comes with the observability and guardrails needed to run it on call without heroics.

Technologies/Methodologies

Kubernetes, Terraform, Helm, GitOps delivery, container build pipelines, Prometheus and Grafana monitoring, and incident runbooks your team actually owns.

Target Audience

Engineering teams outgrowing manual deploys, and platform groups who need a reliable foundation before the next wave of services arrives.

Staff Augmentation

Sometimes you do not need a new team, just the two or three people who can unblock the one you have. Our engineers join your rituals, your repository, and your review process, and they hand over as deliberately as they arrive.

Technologies/Methodologies

Embedded engineers and tech leads, pair programming, documented handover, and whatever stack and process your team already runs on.

Target Audience

Product teams with a committed roadmap and a hiring gap, and CTOs who need specific expertise for one quarter rather than one headcount forever.
